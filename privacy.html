Privacy Policy

Expense AI Tracker · Last updated: November 4, 2026

Expense AI Tracker helps users record and review expenses by sending text messages, bills, or voice notes through WhatsApp. This policy explains what information the application processes and how it is used.

1. Information we process

Depending on how you use the application, it may process:

  • WhatsApp messages and message-related identifiers or metadata made available through the WhatsApp Cloud API.
  • Voice notes or audio files that you choose to send, so their content can be transcribed.
  • Expense details extracted from your messages or audio, such as amounts, dates, descriptions, and categories.
  • Information needed to retrieve and display your expense history.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information through the service.

2. How information is used

Information is used to receive your requests, transcribe voice messages, identify and extract expense details, save expense records, and respond with expense summaries or related information.

3. Service providers

The application uses third-party services to operate:

  • Meta WhatsApp Cloud API to receive and send WhatsApp messages.
  • Gnani AI to transcribe audio when you send a voice note.
  • Google Gemini to interpret messages and assist with expense extraction and responses.
  • Supabase to store and retrieve application data, including expense records.

Information required for a particular feature may be processed by the relevant provider. Those providers handle information under their own terms and privacy policies. Please review their policies for details about their practices.

4. Storage and retention

Expense records and related application data may be stored in the application's Supabase database so that the service can provide expense history and summaries. Message and audio handling may also involve temporary processing by the application and its service providers.

We do not promise a fixed retention period in this policy. Actual retention depends on the application's configuration and the services involved. Avoid sending information you do not want processed or stored.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared with the service providers listed above only as needed to operate the features you use, and may be disclosed where required by law or to protect the service and its users.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect information handled by the application. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Your choices and data requests

You can stop using the service at any time. To ask a question about your information, or to request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with your use of the application, contact us at the email address below. We may need enough information to identify the relevant records and verify the request.

8. Children's privacy

This application is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly design it to collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the application changes. The latest version will be published at this page, with the updated date shown at the top.
